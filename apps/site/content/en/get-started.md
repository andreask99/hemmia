# Get started

There are two ways to use Hemmia. You can start on your phone and move to a hub at home later; your chats come along.

## Just me, on my phone

Install the app, paste your own key from Anthropic or OpenAI, and start chatting. Nothing to set up at home.

[Hemmia on your phone](/guides/phone/)

## My family, at home

Set up a hub on a computer that stays on. It runs local models, gives everyone in the family their own account, and works on every device.

1. [Set up on a Mac](/guides/mac/)
2. [Choosing AI models](/guides/models/)
3. [Invite your family](/guides/family/)

## Which is right for me?

| | On my phone | At home |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you need | A phone and an API key | A computer that stays on |
| Cost | You pay the provider for what you use | Electricity, plus cloud models if you use them |
| Who can use it | Just you | The whole family, on every device |
| Local models | No | Yes |
| Where your chats are stored | On your phone | On the hub |
