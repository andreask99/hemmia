# Hemmia on your phone

You don't need a computer at home to use Hemmia. On its own, the app works with your own API key from Anthropic or OpenAI. There is no account to create, and your chats, photos and key stay on your phone.

<!-- note -->
Your messages go straight from your phone to the provider you chose, under that provider's terms. Hemmia's developer receives nothing: there is no Hemmia account, no server and no analytics.
<!-- /note -->

<!-- steps -->
## Install the app
Hemmia for iPhone is in beta and tested on TestFlight; it needs iOS 15 or later. To join, write to [andreas.apps@icloud.com](mailto:andreas.apps@icloud.com) and you get an invitation by email. The Android app comes later. Want to look around first? The [demo](/demo/?lang=en) shows Hemmia with a made-up family.

## Choose "Just me, on this phone"
On the first screen, tap **Just me, on this phone**.

## Pick a provider
Choose **Anthropic** (Claude models) or **OpenAI** (GPT models). You pay the provider directly for what you use; Hemmia adds nothing. You can add the other provider later in **Settings**.

## Paste your key
Tap **Get a key from Anthropic** (or OpenAI) to open the provider's website, create a key there and copy it. Back in Hemmia, tap **Paste**, then **Check and save**. Hemmia asks the provider whether the key works before it saves it. [Getting an API key](/guides/api-keys/) explains each step.

## Start a chat
Tap **+ New chat**, choose a model and write your message. **Stop** ends a reply early; the part written so far is kept. Turn on **Think harder** for difficult questions: the answer takes longer.
<!-- /steps -->

## Photos

Tap **Add photo** to send up to 4 photos with a message, from the camera or your library. Hemmia makes them smaller before sending (long edge at most 1568 pixels) and removes their location and camera data.

## Your chats

The chat list shows your newest chats first. Use **Search chats** to find one by its title. Tap the menu next to a chat to **Rename** or **Delete** it. A chat's title is the start of its first message until you rename it.

## Settings

Tap **Settings** on the chat list.

- **AI providers:** add a key for the second provider, **Replace** a key or **Remove** it.
- **About me:** a few lines the AI should know about you (your name, your language, what you do). They are sent with every chat.
- **Language:** English or German.
- **Privacy policy** and **Terms of use** open the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy/) and the [terms](/legal/terms/).

## Export, import and delete

Under **Your chats** in Settings:

- **Export chats** saves all chats and photos in one file and opens the share sheet, so you can save it to Files, a computer or a cloud drive.
- **Import chats** reads such a file back. Imported chats are added next to your existing chats; nothing is overwritten.
- **Delete everything on this phone** deletes your chats, photos and keys. Export your chats first if you want to keep them.

<!-- note -->
The export file is not protected. Anyone who has it can read your chats, so keep it somewhere safe.
<!-- /note -->

Your key is not in the export file. On a new phone, paste it again (or create a new key).

## Moving to Hemmia at home

If you set up Hemmia at home later, your chats can come along. Pair the phone with your hub first, then open **Settings → Move to Hemmia at home** and pick the hub. Your chats go to your account on the hub. Your key stays on the phone; the hub uses its own keys. The chats also stay on the phone until you delete them yourself.
