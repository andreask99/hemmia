# Invite your family

Everyone gets their own account with their own passphrase. Admins manage accounts, but nobody except the account owner can read their chats.

<!-- steps -->
## Create an invite
In Hemmia, open **Settings → Family** and tap **Create invite link**. Choose **Admin account** for another parent, or **Teen account (restricted)** for a child.

## Let them scan it, or send the link
Hemmia shows a QR code. The person scans it with their phone's camera, or you tap **Send invite link** to send it through a messenger. The invite works once, for 7 days.

<!-- screenshot name="hub-invite" alt="Settings, Family: a new invite as a QR code, with a Send invite link button and the link itself" -->

## They create their account
They choose a username and a passphrase and get a recovery code to keep somewhere safe.
<!-- /steps -->

<!-- note -->
Invite links work only if other devices can reach your hub: turn on Hemmia Connect or set up Tailscale first.
<!-- /note -->

## Restricted accounts

A restricted account suits teenagers: admins decide whether restricted accounts may use web search and cloud models, under **Settings → Family**. You can restrict or lift the restriction for any account later.

<!-- screenshot name="hub-family" alt="Settings, Family: three accounts, one of them marked Restricted, each with buttons to disable, restrict and see devices" -->

## Devices

Each person can see their devices under **Settings → Account → Devices**, add another one with a QR code, and remove a lost phone. Admins can do the same for others in **Settings → Family**. A removed device is signed out at once and needs a new link to come back.

## Disabling an account

**Disable** stops an account from signing in, without deleting anything. **Enable** brings it back.
