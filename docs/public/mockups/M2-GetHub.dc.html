Hemmia lives on a computer at home

Install the Hemmia hub on a computer that stays on: a Mac with Apple silicon, or a PC, NAS or Raspberry Pi with Docker. Your chats are stored there, encrypted.

1On the computer, open hemmia.app/get and install the hub.
2Follow the setup. At the end it shows a QR code.
3Come back here and scan it.

Opens the share sheet (Mail, AirDrop, Messages).

The hub is ready: scan the QR code
